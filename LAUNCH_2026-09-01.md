### First comment
Hi Product Hunt 👋

I built DEALFAZ around a simple problem: a cheap purchase price alone does not make a good reselling deal.

Instead of showing only one possible resale price, DEALFAZ lets users combine their own numbers for purchase price, realistic selling price, costs, demand and risk. It then shows:

- a transparent DealScore from 0–100
- Buy / Negotiate / Skip
- Worst / Realistic / Best Case
- break-even and safety margin
- a counterargument mode
- sell-through / evidence quality
- capital efficiency for comparing two deals
- a local watchlist without an account

The core tools are free and do not require signup. The current version is focused on the German-speaking DACH market and does not claim proprietary market data or guaranteed profits.

I would especially appreciate feedback on whether the DealScore explanation is clear and which missing input would most improve a real purchase decision.
